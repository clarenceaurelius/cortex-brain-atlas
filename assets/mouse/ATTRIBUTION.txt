Browser derivatives
The original surfaces are simplified by grouping nearby vertices. Triangle
winding is reversed to retain outward orientation after the display transform.
Original coordinates are in micrometres (AP, DV, ML). Display coordinates are
x=(ML-5700)*0.0004, y=(4000-DV)*0.0004, z=(6600-AP)*0.0004.
Thus one display unit represents 2.5 mm. Negative x is the displayed left side.
Each surface is partitioned at the atlas midline for the hemisphere controls.
These are atlas annotation surfaces for an adult mouse reference brain.

Regenerate with scripts/prepare-mouse.py. Structure IDs included in brain.json.gz
are listed in that script. The atlas shell and isocortex provide visual context.
