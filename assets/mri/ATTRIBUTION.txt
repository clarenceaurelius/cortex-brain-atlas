MRI and segmentation: FreeSurfer fsaverage template, obtained from the MNE testing-data distribution.

Repackaged from MGZ into byte arrays; original 1 mm grid and label values retained. Segmentation label numbers converted losslessly from float32 to uint8.
These repackaged data and rendered views are modified derivatives, not original FreeSurfer files.
